## What are Writing Voices?

Writing voices help you maintain consistency and authenticity across your blog posts. Different voices serve different audiences, purposes, or content types. For example:

- A **professional technical voice** for tutorials and technical deep-dives
- A **casual storytelling voice** for personal reflections
- An **academic voice** for research-based content

## Common Voice

**File:** `common-voice.md`

The common voice contains rules that apply to ALL your writing voices. These are your universal guidelines - things you always want or never want regardless of the specific voice you're using.
